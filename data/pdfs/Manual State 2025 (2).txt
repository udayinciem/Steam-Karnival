II. General Rules 
1. One school can represent only one Sahodaya in the State Kalotsav. 
Only students from schools which are affiliated to CBSE and having 
membership in a sahodaya complex shall participate in the State 
Kalotsav. The list of participants for the Kalotsav is to be registered 
by the respective sahodayas. 
2. Any participant/team from a school can register for State Kalotsav only 
through one of the registered Sahodayas.  
3. Website of the Kalotsav -www.steamkarnival.com will be accessible 
from 23 October 2025 
4. Pass word for online registration can be obtained by the sahodaya heads 
by 25 October 2025 from the kalotsav office 
- 
9778665476/9778665475 
5. Online registration for State Kalotsav will start on 23 October 2025. The 
last date for registration is 29 October 2025, and the last date for any 
corrections is 30 October 2025. 
6. One student shall participate in a maximum of five single items and 2 
group items. Out of 5 Single items only 3 can be stage items. However a 
student can participate in 5 off stage items provided he or she is not 
participating in any stage items. 
7. A student from a lower category cannot compete in a higher category and 
vice versa. 
8. Identity Card issued by the organizing committee of the Kalotsav is 
compulsory for participating in any event. 
9. (a) Participation in the following common group items shall be 
irrespective of category or sex.

III. 
GENERAL INSTRUCTIONS REGARDING THE COMPETITIONS 
 
1. In the manual where an item shown as common for boys and girls, means 
that there will be no separate competition for boys and girls. A Group item 
shown as common for boys /girls/ category, means that boys alone or 
girls alone or both boys and girls together irrespective of category shall 
perform the item. 
2. For the items where back stage singing is allowed, singers shall belong to 
the same category and sex. In case of common items, singers shall be 
boys/ girls/ mixed. 
3. In all competitions both stage performers and back stage performers are 
eligible for certificates and prizes. In the case of One Act Play, characters 
and 3 persons for stage arrangements will be given certificate. 
4. In the case of Thiruvathira, Margham Kali and Oppana, both stage 
performers and back stage performers shall wear the same costume and 
make up. 
5. For Ottanthullal, back stage singers can be non-students as well. 
6. For writing and drawing competitions, participants shall bring pen, 
pencil, colour etc. Only paper shall be provided at the venue. The subject/ 
topic shall be decided by the judges. 
7. For elocution, topics shall be given in advance. But for extempore, topic 
shall be decided by the judges and given to the participants five minutes 
before the competition.

IX. PROGRAMME CO-ORDINATION RULES 
1. Core committee that consist of the President, General Secretary and 
Treasurer of C.K.S.C and the convener of the core committee along with the 
members appointed shall be the only official body to decide up on all 
matters regarding the State Kalotsav. 
 
2. Before going to the judgment seat, every judge shall deposit his/her cell 
phone with the judges-in-charge and get a slip against it. 
 
3. The Core committee shall fix the Venue for the State Kalotsav after 
making a physical inspection of the infrastructure availability at the 
venue-school. 
 
4. The venue school shall abide the rules and regulations given in the 
manual of CBSE State Kalotsav. 
 
 
5. The venue school shall collect the participants' fee of Rs.600 from a 
participant and the contribution of Rs.35,000/- from a Sahodaya and 
make the sale of food coupons. The state level organisers and the finance 
committee shall raise necessary funds through sponsors. 
 
6. Banners, posters, arches and other hoardings for the publicity of the State 
Kalotsav shall bear the name of the Kalotsav, venue and date. 
 
7. There shall be a room for the core committee members and for the state 
level office bearers. 
 
8. Food for the Committee members and the judges shall be arranged by the 
host school.

INFORMATION TO THE JUDGES 
1. A separate room is arranged for the judges when they are not involved in 
the judgement. 
 
2. In no case there shall be any public contact. Even talking to the people in 
the premises is to be avoided. 
 
3. Judgment of an item in a particular category will take place at a stretch 
inclusive of all clusters. 
4. There is no separate lunch break. As and when one particular category. 
 
5. If the competition is over, the judges shall be escorted to the mess hall for 
the lunch by the judges-in-charge. If the item prolongs, tiffin will be 
served at the Judgment seat which they consume when a particular 
cluster in a category gets over. 
 
6. After the judgment the tabulation work will be done by the tabulators in 
the presence of the judges and one of the judges will announce the result 
on the stage. 
 
7. There will be three lights on the stage Red, Green and Yellow. Green 
indicates the starting of the item. Yellow indicates there is only one 
minute left. Red indicates the time limit is over. If a participant exceeds 
the time limit, the person is not eligible for any prize. In the stage where 
light indicator is not provided, bell system will be allowed. 
 
8. In case a technical fault occurs before half the time of an item, that event 
will be restarted; but if it happens after the half time, that item can 
performed again at the end of that particular cluster. 
 
 
9. Before starting the judgement, the stage manager will announce the 
profile of the judges in each stage. So, the profiles of the judges are to be 
given early enough to make necessary arrangements.
